## Practice Brief (SG-Proportional for Andrew D.)

For a 2-hour session:

**Approach 150-200yd (35% / 42 min) — SG: -0.9**
1. Random target: vary 150, 160, 170, 180yd targets — never same yardage twice
2. Fairway vs rough: alternate lies to simulate course conditions
3. Pressure set: 5 shots, count how many finish within 30ft of pin

**Short Game 25-50yd (25% / 30 min) — SG: -0.5**
1. Landing spot drill: pick spot halfway to pin, land 10 balls on it
2. Up-and-down challenge: chip and putt, track % saves out of 10
3. Trajectory variation: high soft vs bump-and-run to same target

**Short Game 0-25yd (20% / 24 min) — SG: -0.5**
1. Circle drill: chips must stop inside 6ft circle around pin (10 shots)
2. One-club challenge: pitch with only 56 degree wedge to build touch
3. Greenside bunker: 10 shots, track sand save %

**Putting 10-25ft (15% / 18 min) — SG: -0.5**
1. Lag putting: 10 putts from 20ft, all must stop within 3ft
2. Gate drill: two tees 1 ball-width apart at 4ft, roll 10 through
3. Speed calibration: putt to fringe from 15ft, no backstop
